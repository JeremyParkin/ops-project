kinema — favicon package
========================

Drop these in your site root and paste the HTML below into <head>.

  <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.ico" sizes="32x32">
  <link rel="icon" href="/favicon.svg" type="image/svg+xml">
  <link rel="apple-touch-icon" href="/apple-touch-icon.png">
  <link rel="manifest" href="/site.webmanifest">

Modern browsers take favicon.svg and scale it perfectly. favicon.ico is the
fallback for older browsers and for some feed readers and chat unfurlers.


FILES

favicon.svg          the master. Vector, transparent groove, no background.
favicon.ico          multi-size 16/32/48, transparent
favicon-16/32/48.png transparent, for anywhere that wants a specific size
favicon-192/512.png  transparent, PWA / Android
apple-touch-icon.png 180px, brass on graphite, 14% padding
                     (iOS ignores transparency and adds its own corner radius,
                      so this one needs an opaque ground)
maskable-512.png     brass on graphite, 20% safe-zone inset for Android
                     adaptive icons that crop to a circle or squircle
site.webmanifest     wire-up for the PNGs above
icon-graphite.svg    512 vector, brass on graphite — social avatars, app tiles
icon-paper.svg       512 vector, brass on paper — light-ground avatars


A NOTE ON THE TRANSPARENT GROOVE

favicon.svg and the PNGs leave the groove empty, so it picks up whatever is
behind it — a dark browser tab bar, a light one, either works. The trade is
that against a brass-coloured surface the mark would disappear. If you ever
need it on brass, use icon-graphite.svg instead.

Mark geometry is fixed at all sizes: body plus sliver, groove = stroke 11.5
on r 64 about (12,88), converted to outline. Never re-weight the groove.
Brass is #C8913A everywhere.
